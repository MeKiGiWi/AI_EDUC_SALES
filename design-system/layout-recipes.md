# Layout Recipes

## Default landing recipe

1. Navbar
2. Hero with big claim and green atmospheric decoration
3. Problem framing
4. How it works
5. AI trainer explanation
6. Audience / academy variants
7. Feature and KPI framing
8. Pricing
9. Security / integrations
10. FAQ
11. Final CTA
12. Footer

## Section rhythm

- alternating text-heavy and card-heavy sections
- lots of breathing room
- avoid back-to-back dense grids

## Mobile

- simplify orbit decoration
- keep CTA readable
- collapse complex cards into one-column stacks
