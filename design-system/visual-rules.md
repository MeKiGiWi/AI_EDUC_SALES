# Visual Rules

- Green is the only accent family in this repo.
- Keep the page mostly white and pale green.
- Use deep green for buttons, pills, icons, and selected emphasis.
- Use mint for glows and secondary decorative support.
- Never let decorative effects overpower the copy.
- Avoid default SaaS gradients, purple tints, and blue hero washes.
- Depth should stay soft and low-contrast.
