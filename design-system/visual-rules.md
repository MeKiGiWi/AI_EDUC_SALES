# Visual Rules

## Overall feel

Use a light, minimal, high-trust AI aesthetic. The site should feel like a scientific intelligence layer, not a template.

## Color rules

- 75-90% of the screen should remain white or near-white.
- Use deep navy for primary headings, not pure black.
- Use blue only for emphasis, labels, diagrams, glow, and selected words.
- Gray text can be quite faint, but important body copy must remain readable.
- Do not introduce many secondary colors. Green is allowed only for small status dots. Orange/yellow only for rare metrics.

## Typography rules

- Headings do most of the design work.
- Use fewer words and larger type.
- Use sentence-scale sections: one large sentence can be a whole section.
- Accent words can be blue, but never make every keyword blue.

## Spacing rules

- Use very large vertical whitespace.
- If a section feels empty, it may be correct.
- Do not fill whitespace with icons or extra cards.
- Cards should have room to breathe inside and around them.

## Border and shadow rules

- Borders are pale and precise.
- Shadows are soft and wide, not dark and close.
- Use `rgba(39, 61, 103, 0.08-0.14)` for depth.

## Decorative rules

- Use 2-4 decorative motifs per long page, not dozens.
- Make crystals translucent, slightly blurred, and layered behind content.
- Orbit lines should be thin and almost invisible.
- Diagrams should look like “signal/intelligence” graphics, not charts from a dashboard.

## Anti-patterns

Do not use:

- generic blue SaaS hero gradients
- dark dashboard hero as the default
- emoji-style illustrations
- crowded feature grids
- rectangular cards with small radius
- stock photos
- strong drop shadows
- rainbow gradients
- exact logo/assets/text from the reference
